Tanknote Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: September 28, 2026. Last updated: October 21, 2026.

Tanknote is developed by Guichun (高桂春) to help you log aquarium water parameters, maintenance and livestock. This policy applies to Android version 1.0.0.

The app stores aquarium records and photos locally on your device and does not upload them to developer-operated servers. We do not sell personal information. This version has no Tanknote account registration, cloud sync, ads, analytics SDKs or crash reporting SDKs. User-directed exports, Android system backup and Google Play purchases are described below.

1. Data used on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage:

  • Tank names, types, dimensions and setup dates.
  • Water readings, maintenance logs, reminders, equipment and consumables.
  • Livestock, quarantine and treatment records, including names, quantities, dates and notes you enter.
  • Photos you take or select, tank and livestock icons, and available photo capture dates.
  • Language, theme, units, privacy consent and other settings, and a cached Pro unlock status.

These support aquarium views, charts, albums, reminders and other aquarium tools. The app reads available capture dates from selected photos' EXIF metadata locally to date and sort photos. It does not request device location permission or track your location. Photo copies may retain metadata from the source file; review files before exporting or sharing them.

The developer cannot access your records or photos through the app. Information you choose to email us is handled as described in section 10.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications: maintenance reminders.
  • Exact alarms: scheduling reminders at the requested time.
  • Receive boot completed: restoring reminder schedules after a restart.
  • Vibrate: reminder notifications.
  • Google Play billing: opening Google Play checkout for Tanknote Pro.

Taking or selecting photos uses the system camera or photo picker. Only the selected photo is copied into the app; the app does not browse other photos in your library.

3. Sharing, exports and full backups

When you choose Share, Export CSV / JSON, Back up all data or Care sheet, the app creates an image or file and opens the system share sheet or save interface. You choose the recipient and whether to save locally or to a cloud service. Recipients and storage services handle the content under their own policies.

Full backups include the local database, photos and icon files, and may include records or photo copies marked as deleted. Tanknote does not password-protect or encrypt these backup files. Store them in a trusted location and share them only with intended recipients.

Before restoring a backup, the app keeps a snapshot of the previous data in private storage for Undo last restore. This snapshot may include records and photos. It normally remains until replaced by another restore, removed after an undo, or deleted when you clear app storage.

4. Tanknote Pro purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase. Google Play handles checkout, orders and purchase entitlements. Tanknote does not receive or store your full card number, card security code or similar payment credentials. Google processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

The app uses the Google Play billing service on your phone to query prices, initiate and acknowledge purchases, and check or restore ownership. Purchase results are processed locally and the Pro unlock status is cached on the device; these are not uploaded to developer-operated servers. Google Play may connect to the internet for these operations. Google Play manages refunds, orders and transaction records, which are not deleted when you uninstall Tanknote.

5. System backup and device transfer

If Android system backup or device transfer is enabled, the system may save and restore app data through Google or a device manufacturer's backup service. Availability, storage location, protection and retention depend on your device, settings and service provider. The developer cannot access or delete these backups for you.

This version's backup rules exclude the regular album photo directory and export directory. The database, settings, icons and other eligible private files may still be backed up. In particular, the pre-restore snapshot described in section 3 may contain photos, so system backups are not guaranteed to exclude all photos.

Manage system backup and device transfer in your device settings. System backup is separate from the full backups you export yourself. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete exported files or existing system backups.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local records and settings remain on the device until replaced or removed through actions such as clearing app storage or uninstalling.
  • Some in-app delete actions mark data as deleted: it disappears from normal lists, but database records or photo copies may remain locally and may be included in later backups. This is not immediate, permanent file erasure.
  • To remove data in the app's private storage on the current device, use Android settings to Clear storage / Clear data for Tanknote, or uninstall it. On reinstallation, the system may restore earlier data according to your backup settings.
  • Manage and delete exported files, full backups, copies sent to others, system backups and Google Play orders separately in the relevant location or service. Deleting a photo in Tanknote does not delete the original from your system photo library.
  • This version has no Tanknote accounts or developer-hosted aquarium data, so there is no Tanknote account to close.

7. Children

Tanknote is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-party services and security

The app has no ads, analytics SDKs or crash reporting SDKs. This version does not request internet access permission and does not directly upload aquarium records, photos or diagnostic logs to network servers. Google Play billing, Android system backup and file storage or sharing services you choose are handled by their respective providers, rather than by developer-operated servers.

Local data uses Android's private app storage protections. Tanknote does not add app-level encryption to its database, photos or exported backups. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported files secure.

9. Changes

If online features or data handling change, we will update this policy and provide in-app notices or seek consent as applicable. Planned future features are not part of this version.

10. Contact

Developer: Guichun (高桂春). For privacy questions, email 1039438475@qq.com.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you send. We use them only to respond to and handle your inquiry, and retain them for the time needed to resolve it and provide necessary follow-up. Do not send card security codes, passwords or unrelated personal information. You may request deletion of correspondence using the same email address.

Tanknote 隐私政策

生效日期:2026 年 9 月 28 日。最近更新:2026 年 10 月 21 日。

Tanknote 由 Guichun(高桂春)开发,是一款记录鱼缸水质、维护和生物的工具。本政策适用于 Android 1.0.0 版本。

养缸记录和照片由 App 保存在设备本地,Tanknote 不向开发者服务器上传这些内容,不出售个人信息。 本版本不提供 Tanknote 账号注册或云同步,不包含广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。用户主动导出、Android 系统备份及 Google Play 付款涉及的数据处理分别见下文。

1. App 会存哪些数据

你在 App 里填写的内容,全部保存在手机上本 App 的私有存储里:

  • 鱼缸信息:名称、缸型、尺寸、开缸日期
  • 水质读数、维护记录、提醒设置、设备与耗材
  • 生物记录、检疫和治疗记录:名称、数量、日期及你填写的备注等
  • 你拍摄或选择的照片(鱼缸相册、鱼缸和生物的图标),以及可读取的照片拍摄日期
  • 语言、主题、单位、隐私政策同意状态等设置,以及本机缓存的 Pro 解锁状态

这些内容用于展示鱼缸信息、趋势、相册、维护提醒和其他养缸工具。App 会在本机读取所选照片中的拍摄日期信息(EXIF),用于记录和排序;不会请求设备定位权限或追踪你的位置。照片副本可能保留原文件中的元数据,导出或分享前请自行确认内容。

开发者无法通过 App 访问你的养缸记录或照片。你主动通过邮件向我们提供的内容,按第 10 节处理。

2. 权限用途

权限用途
通知到点提醒你换水、测水质等维护任务
精确闹钟让提醒准时响,不被系统推迟
开机自启(接收开机广播)手机重启后恢复已设好的提醒
震动提醒通知响起时震动
Google Play 结算购买 Tanknote Pro 时调起 Google Play 的付款界面

拍照和从相册选图使用系统自带的相机和照片选择器,只有你选中的那张照片会被拷贝进 App,App 不会读取你相册里的其他内容。

3. 分享与导出

只有在你主动点击「分享缸况」「导出 CSV / JSON」「备份全部数据」或「度假说明」时,App 才会生成对应的图片或文件,并交给系统分享面板或文件保存界面。发给谁、保存到手机还是云盘由你决定;接收方或云盘服务会按其自身政策处理内容。

完整备份包含本地数据库和照片、图标文件,也可能包含已标记删除的记录或照片副本。备份文件没有由 Tanknote 设置密码或加密,请保存在可信位置,避免分享给不应看到这些内容的人。

恢复备份前,App 会在私有存储中保留一份恢复前的数据快照,用于「撤销上次恢复」。快照可能包含记录和照片,通常保留到下次恢复时被覆盖、执行撤销后被移除,或你清除应用存储时被删除。

4. 购买 Tanknote Pro

Pro 是一次性购买,由 Google Play 处理付款、订单及购买资格。Tanknote 不接收或保存你的完整银行卡号、安全码等支付凭据。 Google 会按其服务条款和隐私政策处理付款所需的信息。

App 通过手机上的 Google Play 结算服务查询商品价格、发起和确认购买,以及核对和恢复购买资格;返回的购买结果由 App 在本机处理,并缓存 Pro 解锁状态,不上传到开发者服务器。Google Play 可能联网完成这些操作。退款、订单及相关交易记录由 Google Play 管理,不会随着卸载 Tanknote 被一并删除。

5. 系统备份

如果设备启用了 Android 系统备份或换机迁移,系统可能通过 Google 或设备厂商提供的备份服务保存和恢复 App 数据。备份的可用性、存储位置、保护方式和保留时间取决于设备、系统设置及服务提供方,开发者无法访问或替你删除这些备份。

本版本的备份规则排除了普通相册照片目录和导出目录,但数据库、设置、图标及其他允许备份的私有文件仍可能被备份。其中,第 3 节所述恢复前快照可能包含照片,因此不能保证系统备份完全不含照片。

你可以在设备的系统备份设置中管理备份和换机迁移。系统备份与用户主动导出的完整备份是两种不同方式;删除 App 不会自动删除你已导出的文件或系统已经保存的备份。

6. 删除数据

  • 本地记录和设置在设备上保留,直到被覆盖、清除应用存储或卸载等操作移除。
  • App 中的部分「删除」采用标记删除方式:内容不再显示在正常列表中,但对应数据库记录或照片副本可能继续保留在本机,并可能包含在后续备份中。这不等于立即、彻底擦除文件。
  • 要清除当前设备上 App 私有存储中的数据,可在 Android 设置中对 Tanknote 执行「清除存储 / 清除数据」,或卸载 App。重新安装时,系统可能根据你的备份设置恢复此前的数据。
  • 你已保存的导出文件、完整备份、发给他人的副本、系统备份及 Google Play 订单,需要分别在对应位置或服务中管理和删除。删除 App 内的照片不会删除你系统相册中的原图。
  • 本版本没有 Tanknote 账号及开发者服务器上的养缸数据,因此没有需要注销的 Tanknote 账号。

7. 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意向 13 岁以下儿童索取个人信息。

8. 第三方服务

App 不包含广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。本版本不申请互联网访问权限,不直接向网络服务器上传养缸记录、照片或运行诊断日志。Google Play 结算、Android 系统备份以及你主动选择的文件保存或分享服务,由相应的系统或第三方服务处理,不属于向开发者服务器上传数据。

本地数据使用 Android 的应用私有存储保护,但 Tanknote 没有为数据库、照片或导出的备份另行设置应用级加密。请使用设备锁屏等保护措施,并妥善保管导出文件。
